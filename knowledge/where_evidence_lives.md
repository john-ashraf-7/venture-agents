Write queries that find buyers, not designers or vendors. Problem statements live where people talk in their own voice: Reddit and niche forums, Stack Exchange and Quora questions, community discussion boards, product reviews. Add words people use when stuck: "how do I", "struggling with", "any template for", "recommend", plus the buyer ("for small bakeries", "for freelancers").
Prices live on marketplaces and storefronts: Etsy listings, Gumroad, Creative Market, app stores, "pricing" pages of named products. Query the product noun with the marketplace name (e.g. "<product> template etsy").
Avoid ambiguous words that pull in other meanings (e.g. "pricing page" returns pricing-page designs). One query per evidence type: forum problem, priced product, trend with a figure.
