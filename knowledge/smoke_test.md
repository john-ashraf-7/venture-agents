---
id: smoke_test
agents: [experiment_designer, landing_page]
source: Common lean-startup practice (fake-door / landing-page tests)
---
A smoke test is a real page describing the offer honestly, with a waitlist or pre-order form, shown to the target buyer. Decide before launch: the sign-up threshold, the minimum number of visitors, and the time window. Count only real visitors from the target channel; exclude friends, team members and demo traffic. The page must not claim the product exists if it does not, and must not invent social proof or urgency.
