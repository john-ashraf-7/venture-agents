Ask about the person's past behaviour, not about your idea. Good questions: "When did this last happen?", "What did you do about it?", "What have you tried or paid for?", "What would you lose if it stayed this way?". Bad questions: "Would you buy...?", "Do you like this idea?", hypotheticals and compliments. Commitment (time, money, introductions) is evidence; praise is not. Write down exact words and dates.
