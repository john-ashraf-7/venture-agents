---
Rank assumptions by impact x uncertainty and test the riskiest one first, usually "this buyer has this problem and will pay to solve it". Fill the Lean Canvas from evidence where it exists and mark every other box as an assumption. Do not spend build time before the riskiest assumption has a pass/fail test with a number decided in advance.
