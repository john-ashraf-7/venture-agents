Warning signs at the evidence stage: no person describing the problem in their own words; no one paying for any alternative; a market defined as "everyone"; motivation stated only as "fast money"; effort or cost far above the founder's budget or time; dozens of free equivalents. Positive signs: dated complaints from the same buyer type, priced competitors with reviews, a narrow buyer the founder can reach.
