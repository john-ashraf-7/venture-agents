"Everyone" is not a market. A first digital product should serve one buyer with one recurring job in one channel, e.g. "invoice reminder templates for freelance designers paid late", not "productivity templates". Signs an idea is too broad: target market of "anyone", dozens of free alternatives, no specific moment of pain. Narrowing usually raises willingness to pay and makes evidence easier to find.
