source: Common digital-product pricing practice (value-based pricing; good/better/best tiers)
---
Anchor price on visible competitor prices and on the value of the job done, not on production cost. For template packs and guides, two or three tiers (basic, complete, complete plus support or updates) let buyers self-select. State exactly what is included, the licence, and the refund policy. Never show fake discounts, fake "was" prices or countdowns. Any revenue figure is a scenario labelled ASSUMPTION, never a promise.
