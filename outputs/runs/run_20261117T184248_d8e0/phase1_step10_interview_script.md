# Mom Test interview script

Goal: Understand how users currently manage productivity tasks and the costs or time they invest in existing solutions

1. What tools or methods do you currently use to keep track of tasks or projects?
2. Can you walk me through a recent project and how you organized the tasks?
3. What was the most frustrating part of that process?
4. How much time do you spend each week setting up or maintaining your task system?
5. Have you tried any paid productivity tools? If so, what did you pay for and what did you use it for?
6. What features do you wish your current tools had that would make your workflow smoother?
7. How often do you find yourself creating custom templates or notes to fit your needs?
8. What is the biggest time sink in managing your tasks each day?
9. Have you ever abandoned a tool because it was too complex or cluttered? What happened?
10. What would make you consider switching to a new task management solution?
