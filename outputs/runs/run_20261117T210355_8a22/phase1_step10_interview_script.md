# Mom Test interview script

Goal: Understand how busy individuals currently manage productivity and what tools they use or pay for.

1. What was the last time you had to create a to‑do list from scratch? When did that happen and how long did it take you?
2. Can you walk me through the steps you take to organize a day’s tasks? What tools or methods do you use?
3. What tools or templates have you tried to speed up task planning? Which ones did you actually use and why?
4. Tell me about a time you felt a productivity tool didn’t fit your workflow. What did you do instead?
5. How much time or money have you spent on productivity tools or templates in the past six months? Can you give specific examples?
6. When you’re overwhelmed, how do you decide which tasks to focus on? What criteria or tools help you prioritize?
7. What is the most frustrating part of creating a daily plan for yourself? How do you currently cope with that frustration?
8. Have you ever paid for a productivity resource (e.g., a paid template, app subscription, or course)? What prompted you to spend on it, and how did it work out?
9. How do you track the effectiveness of the productivity methods you use? What metrics or feedback loops do you rely on?
10. What would you say is the biggest obstacle that keeps you from getting a quick, ready‑to‑use productivity plan?
