# Market brief

The market for generic to‑do list templates includes free printable options such as Calendarkart.com’s templates [C3] and GoSkills’ Excel templates [C4], as well as paid digital platforms like Notion’s Personal Task HQ ($9) and Ultimate Brain 3.0 ($129) [C25][C26]. Microsoft Excel offers free project planning and to‑do list templates online [C28]. Productivity experts claim that working from a list can boost output by up to 25% [C5], yet users often report that rigid lists can stifle creativity [C31]. Digital tools such as Trello provide integrated calendar views and checklists for subtasks [C37], complementing physical solutions like the Tackle Box’s sticky pad system [C35][C36].
