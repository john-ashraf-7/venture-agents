# Mom Test interview script

Goal: Understand how new freelance designers and writers currently handle client payments, what workarounds they use, and how much they have already invested in managing payments.

1. Can you walk me through the last time you sent a payment reminder to a client? What did you do and what happened?
2. What tools or methods do you currently use to track invoices and follow up on overdue payments? How did you decide on those?
3. Tell me about a time when a client missed a payment deadline. How did you handle it and what was the outcome?
4. What is the typical amount of time you spend each week managing invoices and payment reminders?
5. Have you spent any money on software, templates, or services to help with invoicing or payment collection? If so, what did you buy and why?
6. What challenges do you face when trying to get clients to pay on time?
7. Describe a recent situation where you had to send an unprofessional reminder. What was the impact?
8. How do you decide when to use a formal invoice versus a simple email?
9. What costs (time, money, effort) do you associate with missed payments?
10. What is the most frustrating part of dealing with late payments for you?
