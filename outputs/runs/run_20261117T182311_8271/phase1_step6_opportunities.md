# Opportunities (Phase 1, step 6)

## Theory of value

- Who: New freelance designers and writers who are just starting out
- Problem: They struggle to manage client payments, often missing deadlines and sending unprofessional reminders, which leads to cash flow issues and strained relationships
- Why now: The freelance economy is growing rapidly, with many creators launching services on platforms like Upwork, Fiverr, and personal websites. Early career freelancers need quick, reliable tools to establish professional workflows before they scale
- Why us: The founder has 6 years of copywriting experience and firsthand knowledge of freelance challenges, enabling the creation of clear, jargon-free templates and a practical invoicing checklist
