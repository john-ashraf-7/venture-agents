# Market brief

Late payments are a major issue for freelancers, and research shows that payment reminders significantly reduce late payments [C5]. Sending the first reminder 3 to 5 days before the invoice is due boosts payment likelihood by about 12% versus 4% without a reminder [C9, C10]. A correctly formatted invoice can cut average payment time from 35 days to 10‑14 days [C8]. Each follow‑up increases the chance of payment, with most invoices paid by the fourth reminder [C11, C12]. Invoice errors account for 61% of late payments, underscoring the importance of clear, professional templates [C7]. Scheduling reminders on Monday or Tuesday optimizes response rates [C13].
