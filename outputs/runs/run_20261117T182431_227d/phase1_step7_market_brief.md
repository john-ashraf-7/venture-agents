# Market brief

The market for payment‑reminder templates and invoicing checklists targets new freelance designers and writers, a group that often faces late payments due to overlooked details or lost invoices [C3]. A concise, goodwill‑oriented reminder—listing invoice number, amount, due date, attachment, and a single payment method—has proven effective in prompting timely payment [C2]. The templates strike the right tone, firm enough to prompt action yet professional enough to preserve client relationships [C4]. Common invoice delays stem from missing client details, unclear descriptions, wrong totals, missing due dates, or vague payment instructions; a short pre‑send checklist addresses these friction points [C6][C7][C8]. Because many invoices still arrive with errors, a clear reminder can resolve most late‑payment issues that are not due to bad clients but to forgetfulness or administrative delays [C9][C10].
