# Mom Test interview script

Goal: Interview with new freelance designers and writers to understand how they handle invoicing, payment collection, and related costs.

1. Can you walk me through the last time you sent an invoice to a client? What steps did you take from drafting to sending?
2. When a client delays payment, what actions do you usually take to follow up? How long does it typically take before you get a response?
3. Have you ever used any tools or services to manage invoices or payment reminders? Which ones, and how much did you spend on them?
4. What has been the most frustrating part of collecting payments from clients? Can you describe a specific incident that illustrates this?
5. When you receive a late payment, how do you adjust your own budgeting or cash flow for that month? What costs or adjustments do you have to make?
6. Can you tell me about a time when a client disputed an invoice? What steps did you take to resolve it, and what was the outcome?
7. What do you do when a client cancels a project before payment? How do you handle the remaining invoices or deposits?
8. How do you keep track of which invoices are paid, pending, or overdue? What system or method do you use?
9. Have you ever had to write a late fee or penalty clause into an invoice? How did you decide on the amount and communicate it to the client?
10. When you first started freelancing, how did you learn about invoicing best practices? Who or what influenced your approach?
