# Mom Test interview script

Goal: Understand how new freelance designers and writers currently manage unpaid invoices, what workarounds they use, and how much time or money they have already invested in these processes.

1. When was the last time you had to chase an unpaid invoice from a client? What did you do to follow up?
2. Can you walk me through the steps you take each time you send a reminder? Which tools or templates do you use?
3. What time does it usually take you from sending the initial invoice to receiving payment? How many days have you had to wait?
4. Have you ever paid for any service, software, or template to help with invoice follow‑ups? If so, how much did you spend and what did you get?
5. What was the most frustrating part of dealing with unpaid invoices for you? How did you cope with that frustration?
6. When you finally get paid, how do you measure the impact on your cash flow or day‑to‑day work?
7. Have you ever had to negotiate a payment plan or partial payment with a client? What was the outcome?
8. What do you do when a client refuses to pay or is unresponsive? Do you have a standard response or a different approach?
9. How often do you find yourself re‑writing or re‑sending invoices because the original was incorrect or incomplete?
10. What would you say is the biggest time or money loss you experience because of unpaid invoices?

## Avoid

- Asking whether they would buy the pack (hypothetical; ask what they did last time instead)
- Pitching the idea or describing the product before asking about their past behaviour
- Leading questions that suggest the answer ("Isn't chasing payments stressful?")
