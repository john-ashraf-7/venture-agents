# Compliance checklist

Not legal advice - confirm with a lawyer in your jurisdiction.

| item | applies | source | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ensure all personal data collected through the template pack complies with applicable data protection laws (e.g., GDPR for EU customers, Egypt PDPL for Egyptian customers). | True | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj | Collect only necessary data and provide a privacy notice. |
| Provide a clear 14‑day withdrawal right for digital content under the EU Consumer Rights Directive. | True | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj | Offer a refund within 14 days of purchase if the product is not as described. |
| Comply with the US CAN‑SPAM Act when sending marketing emails about the template pack. | True | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business | Include a valid physical address and an opt‑out mechanism in all promotional emails. |
| If you use endorsements or testimonials in your marketing, follow FTC guidelines for endorsements and reviews. | True | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255 | Disclose any material connections between you and endorsers. |
| If you display consumer reviews, ensure they are truthful and not misleading per FTC review rules. | True | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-465 | Do not fabricate or manipulate reviews. |
| Apply EU VAT rules for digital services if you sell to EU customers. | True | https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/index_en | Register for the One‑Stop Shop if required. |
| Make the template pack accessible to users with disabilities in accordance with WCAG 2.2. | True | https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ | Use accessible file formats (e.g., PDF with tags). |
