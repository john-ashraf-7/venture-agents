# Compliance checklist

Not legal advice - confirm with a lawyer in your jurisdiction.

| item | applies | source | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ensure that any personal data collected from customers (e.g., email addresses for sending templates) is processed in compliance with applicable data protection laws such as the EU GDPR and Egypt PDPL. | True | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj | Collect only necessary data, provide a privacy notice, and obtain consent where required. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
| Provide a clear 14‑day withdrawal or refund policy in line with the EU Consumer Rights Directive and Egypt Consumer Protection Law, and display it prominently on the sales page. | True | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj | The policy must be easily accessible and written in the customer’s language. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
| When using any testimonials or endorsements in marketing materials, follow the US FTC guidelines (ftc_endorsements and ftc_fake_reviews) to ensure disclosures are clear and truthful. | True | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255 | Disclose any material connections and avoid fabricated reviews. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
| Comply with the US CAN‑SPAM Act when sending promotional emails, including providing an opt‑out mechanism and accurate sender information. | True | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business | Include a clear unsubscribe link in every email. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
| If selling to EU customers, apply the EU VAT rules for digital services and register for the One‑Stop Shop if necessary. | True | https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/index_en | Calculate VAT correctly and issue tax invoices. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
| Ensure that any downloadable templates or checklists are accessible to users with disabilities, following WCAG 2.2 guidelines. | True | https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ | Use accessible PDF formats and provide alt text for images. Not legal advice – confirm with a lawyer. |
