# Opportunities (Phase 1, step 6)

## Theory of value

- Who: Everyone who wants to be productive
- Problem: Productivity tools are often generic, cluttered, or hard to customize; users struggle to find a ready‑made, simple to-do list that fits their workflow
- Why now: Digital productivity tools are booming as remote work expands; users look for quick, low‑effort solutions that can be downloaded and used immediately
- Why us: We can produce a lightweight, high‑quality template pack quickly with a low budget, leveraging existing design templates and minimal content creation time
